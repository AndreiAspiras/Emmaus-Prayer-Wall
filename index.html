The Emmaus idea

Sometimes the first answer is simply knowing you're not walking alone.

In Luke 24, two disciples walked toward Emmaus carrying grief and confusion. Jesus came near and walked with them. Emmaus carries that same idea into a simple online space: bring what you carry, and let others walk with you in prayer.
