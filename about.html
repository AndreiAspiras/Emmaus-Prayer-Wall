Why “Emmaus”?

A road. Two travelers. A presence they didn't recognize.

The story behind the name is also the story behind the purpose of this place.

Luke 24:13–35

The road to Emmaus

After the resurrection, two disciples were walking away from Jerusalem toward Emmaus. They carried grief, confusion, and questions.

Then Jesus came near. They did not recognize Him at first, but He walked with them, listened, and opened the Scriptures.

01

The walk

They were moving forward while carrying what had happened behind them. Sometimes faith looks like continuing down the road even when you don't have all the answers.

02

The presence

Jesus drew near and went with them. His presence came before their recognition.

“And it came to pass, that, while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus himself drew near, and went with them.” Luke 24:15 · King James Version
03

The recognition

At the breaking of bread, their eyes were opened. Looking back, they realized something had been happening on the road all along.

“Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way?” Luke 24:32 · King James Version

Our purpose

Emmaus is a place to bring what you carry.

You share what's heavy; someone else chooses to carry a piece of it with you in prayer. We don't promise instant answers. We simply make room for people to walk together.

Community guidelines

Sharing well, together.

A prayer wall works best when it is treated as a place of trust.

01

Be honest, be brief.

A few sentences is enough. You don't need to explain everything.

02

Protect privacy.

Avoid identifying details about someone else without their consent.

03

Stay respectful.

This is a space for encouragement, not debate or judgment.

04

Anonymous is welcome.

You never have to share your name to be prayed for.

05

Praying counts.

If you tap “I'm praying,” take a moment to actually mean it.

06

Use wisdom.

Emmaus is a prayer community, not emergency or professional crisis support.
